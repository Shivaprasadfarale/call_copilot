# Email Templates — Innovate Marketers

> **These leads DON'T remember replying. Treat every email like they're hearing from you for the FIRST time.**
> Keep it under 5 lines. If they have to scroll, they won't read it.

I found the reason why. Want me to send it over?

Shiv
(645) 220-1660

---

> **Why this works:**
> - No mention of colleagues, previous emails, or anything they don't remember
> - Opens with something about THEIR business, not about you
> - "I found the reason why" = curiosity — they NEED to know
> - "Want me to send it over?" = dead simple yes/no
> - 3 lines total

## RULES

1. **NEVER mention colleagues, previous emails, or "you replied to us."** They don't remember. You'll sound like spam.

2. **5 lines MAX.** Phone screen = small. If they scroll, you're dead.

3. **First line = about THEIR business.** Not "Hi, I'm Shiv from Innovate Marketers." Nobody cares who you are yet.

4. **NEVER say:** "completely free" / "no charges" / "no obligation" / "subject-matter expert" / "authority score" / "referring domains"

5. **Always name their competitor.** Google "[their service] [their city]" — whoever shows up #1 is their competitor. Use that name. It makes it personal and impossible to ignore.

6. **CTA = yes or no.** Not "what day works best?" Just: "Interested?" / "Want me to show you?" / "Worth a look?"

7. **Same thread.** Always reply in the existing thread, never start a new email.

8. **No screenshots in email.** Save that for the screen share. Screenshots in emails = spam filter.

Google "[their service] in [their city]" and note:
