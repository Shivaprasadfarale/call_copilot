## 1. High-Converting Audit & Reply Follow-Up Template

This template is designed for following up with prospects who previously replied or asked for information. It uses an anti-spam quotation technique and leverages a Semrush audit screenshot to drive curiosity and get a response.

### Anti-Spam Guidelines:
1. **Spaced Links & Emails**: Always add spaces in domain links and email addresses in the quoted header (e.g., `www. nanoarchitech . com` and `user @ domain . com`) to avoid triggering spam / phishing filters.
2. **Reply Bait CTA**: Do not attach heavy PDFs in the first email. Ask them to reply first so subsequent reports land in their Primary Inbox rather than Spam.
