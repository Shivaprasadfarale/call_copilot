## 2. When to Recommend Which Package

### A. Local SEO Packages ($500 – $1,200 / month)
* **Who it is for**: Businesses that rely entirely on customers walking into a physical location or service providers serving a specific metro zip code.
* **Examples**: Plumbers, roofers, dentists, law firms, chiropractors, restaurants, auto repair shops.
* **Key Pitch Angle**: *"If your customers search 'near me' or look for you on Google Maps, Local SEO gets you in the top 3 Map Pack where 70% of phone calls happen."*

### B. Comprehensive SEO Packages ($599 – $2,499 / month)
* **Who it is for**: Regional, national, or e-commerce businesses that want organic keyword rankings across Google Search, authority link building, and content marketing.
* **Examples**: Regional B2B firms, online stores, software companies, medical tourism, multi-city contractors.
* **Key Pitch Angle**: *"Local SEO only helps on maps. Comprehensive SEO builds website authority, ranks you for dozens of high-value keywords, produces monthly blog articles and backlinks, and drives national organic search volume."*

### C. Enterprise SEO Packages ($2,999 – $4,499 / month)
* **Who it is for**: Major corporations, competitive industries (finance, crypto, legal, national e-commerce), or companies that need high backlink velocity and AI search dominance (AEO & GEO).
* **Key Pitch Angle**: *"In high-stakes markets, standard SEO is not aggressive enough. Enterprise SEO deploys up to 800 backlinks, 8 guest posts, 4 paid PR distributions, and optimizes your site so AI tools like Google Gemini and ChatGPT cite your brand."*

### D. PPC / Google Ads ($300 – $800 / month + Ad Spend)
* **Who it is for**: Businesses that need immediate phone calls and leads starting tomorrow, while organic SEO builds over 3–6 months.
* **Key Pitch Angle**: *"SEO is your long-term equity, but PPC is your instant switch. We capture people searching with high intent right now while your organic rankings climb."*

### E. Google Voice SEO ($399 – $1,199 / month)
* **Who it is for**: Modern businesses, local providers, and tech-savvy brands wanting to capture zero-click voice assistant searches (Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant).
* **Key Pitch Angle**: *"Over 50% of smartphone searches are now spoken out loud. When someone asks Siri or Google 'Who is the best plumber near me?', voice SEO ensures your business is the single spoken answer."*
