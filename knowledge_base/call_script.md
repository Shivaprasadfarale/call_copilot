# Call Script — Innovate Marketers

> **Keep this open during every call. Even if AI is helping, this is your safety net.**
> Replace [CLIENT] with their name, [BUSINESS] with their business name.

---

## PHASE 1 — OPENING (first 30 seconds)

### Standard Opening:
> "Hey [CLIENT], this is Shiv from Innovate Marketers. My colleague had reached out to you about your website and you had replied. Just following up — do you have a quick minute?"

### If They Don't Remember:
> "No worries — we took a look at [BUSINESS]'s website and noticed a few things that might be holding you back. We just wanted to share what we found."

### If They Ask "Who Are You?":
> "We're a digital marketing agency based out of Miami. We help businesses like yours get found on Google. My colleague emailed you about your website — I'm just following up on that."

### If It Goes to Voicemail:
> "Hey [CLIENT], it's Shiv from Innovate Marketers following up on the website report you requested from my colleague. Feel free to call me back on my direct line at (645) 220-1660. Again, that's Shiv at (645) 220-1660. Talk soon."

---

## PHASE 2 — FIND THE PAIN (ask questions, DON'T pitch)

Pick 1-2 of these. Listen to their answers. Mirror what they say.

> "How's business going? Are you getting enough leads coming in from online?"

> "Are you handling the website and SEO yourself or do you have someone doing it?"

> "Where are most of your customers finding you right now — word of mouth, Google, social media?"

### Mirror Technique (repeat their last few words as a question):
- They say: "Business has been slow lately"
- You say: "Business has been slow? What do you think is causing that?"

- They say: "I've been trying to do SEO myself"
- You say: "Doing it yourself? How's that been going so far?"

---

## PHASE 3 — EDUCATE (use their actual data)

> **This is where you become the doctor showing the X-ray. Use SPECIFIC numbers from Semrush.**

### Template:

> "So I pulled up your website before this call. A few things stood out:"

> "Your authority score is just [X] out of 100. That's how Google rates your website's trustworthiness."

> "Right now you're ranking for only [X] keywords. That means when people search for [their service] in [their city], they're not finding you."

> "Your organic traffic is [X] visitors per month. That's [people/nobody] finding you through Google."

> "I can see you have [X] backlinks. [If from Fiverr: 'Some of these are from Fiverr — those are AI-generated links that actually hurt your ranking.']"

> "The reason I'm reaching out is to give you the right strategy. Whether you do it yourself or give us a chance to show you what we can do."

### If They Say "I Already Know That":
> "That's great — you're already ahead of most people. But if the numbers still aren't moving, something's off in the execution. That's what our expert can pinpoint."

### If They Ask "What Exactly Is Wrong?":
> "Main things:
> 1. Your pages aren't optimized for what people actually search — like '[service] near me'
> 2. Your backlinks need work — quality matters more than quantity
> 3. Google can't read your content properly — there are technical issues"

---

## PHASE 4 — SUGGEST THE MEETING (once, softly)

> **Only say this AFTER educating. NOT in the first 2 minutes.**

> "Here's what I'd suggest — we have a senior expert who can pull up your website live on screen share, walk you through everything, and give you a clear roadmap. Takes 15 minutes. Completely free. Even if you never work with us, that info is yours."

> "If you like it, we can talk next steps. If not, no hard feelings. Fair enough?"

### Getting the Time:
> "What day works for you? And what time?"
> "Perfect. I'll send you a Google Meet invite right now."

### If They're Free NOW:
> "Perfect — let me grab our expert. Can you hold one minute?"

---

## PHASE 5 — HANDLING OBJECTIONS

### "I don't need help / doing it myself"
> "I respect that. But if the numbers aren't moving after a few months, something in the approach might need adjusting. Our expert can validate what you're doing — think of it as a free second opinion."

### "I don't have money"
> "The meeting is completely free. No payment, no commitment. We just want to show you where you stand."

### "I've been burned before"
> "Most of our clients were burned before. That's why we don't do contracts — month-to-month. If we don't deliver, fire us."

### "Send me an email"
> "I can do that. But an email is just numbers — it doesn't show you why competitors are outranking you. 15 minutes on screen share and you'll see everything."

### "I'm busy"
> "Totally get it. What does your calendar look like later this week? Just 15 minutes."

### "How much does it cost?"
> "Depends on what you need — every business is different. But this meeting is free. Pricing only comes up if you want it to."

### "3-6 months is too long"
> "SEO is like when you first started your business — you didn't earn much the first month either, right? But once things started rolling, revenue grew. SEO compounds the same way."

### "I don't do Google Meet"
> "No problem — what works? WhatsApp? Phone call? We'll make it work."

### "I want YOU, not your colleague"
> "I'll be right there on the call. My expert just handles the technical walkthrough."

### "My website is new"
> "That's actually the best time to get this right. Better to set the foundation now than rebuild later."

---

## RECOVERY LINES (when things go sideways)

### Awkward Silence:
> "So yeah... anyway, the main thing I wanted to share was what we found about your website."

### Lost Track:
> "Sorry — where were we? You were saying..."

### Client Gets Upset:
> "I hear you, and I don't want to waste your time. Just wanted to share what we found."

### Need to Buy Time:
> "Right, so..." / "Okay, got it..." / "That makes sense..."

### Don't Know the Answer:
> "That's exactly the kind of thing our expert can answer in detail during the walkthrough."

### Client Says Something Unexpected:
> "Okay, tell me more about that."
