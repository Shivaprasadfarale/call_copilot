## 1. Overview & Pricing Tiers

Local SEO packages are specifically designed for physical brick-and-mortar storefronts, professional practices (lawyers, doctors, accountants), and local service-area businesses (contractors, plumbers, roofers). The packages are structured according to the number of physical business locations or service territories.

| Tier Name | Monthly Investment | Locations Covered | GBP Posts / Month | Local Citations & Link Building |

| **Basic** | **$500 / month** | 1 Location | 4 GBP Posts | 2 Local Listings, 1 Blog Post, 5 Blog Marketing, 3 Classifieds |
| **Standard** | **$700 / month** | 2 Locations | 6 GBP Posts | 3 Local Listings, 1 Blog Post, 10 Blog Marketing, 6 Classifieds |
